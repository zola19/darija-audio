Privacy Policy

Darija Tutor (Android app) · Effective date: 7 October 2026

In short: Darija Tutor has no accounts and we (the developer) do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or messages. Your learning progress stays on your phone. The app shows optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device data to show and measure those ads, as explained below.

1. Who we are

Darija Tutor is developed by ZoraDev ("we", "us"). Contact: zahradevelopper@gmail.com.

2. Information stored only on your device

The app saves the following on your phone so it can work offline. It is never sent to us:

  • the language you chose for the app;
  • your learning progress (cards, quiz results) and the lessons you have unlocked;
  • the downloaded audio, picture and video files used by the lessons.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android Settings, deletes all of this.

3. Downloading lesson content

On first launch, and when new lessons are available, the app downloads lesson files (audio, pictures and a small settings file) from our file hosting provider (GitHub) and, for videos, from our video hosting provider. Like any website, these providers receive your device's IP address and basic technical information (such as the time of the request) in order to deliver the files. We do not use this information to identify you.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Some lessons can be unlocked by choosing to watch a short rewarded ad. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID and other device identifiersAdvertising, ad measurement, fraud prevention
IP address and approximate location derived from itAdvertising, fraud prevention
App interactions with ads (e.g. ad views, clicks)Advertising, analytics
Diagnostics (e.g. crash logs, performance) of the ads SDKKeeping ads working

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy, and explains how it uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices:

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings → Privacy settings.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Watching ads is always optional. You can use the free lessons without watching any ad.

5. Permissions

  • Internet / network state: to download lessons and show ads.
  • Advertising ID: used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described above.

The app does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or precise location.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. We do not share data with anyone except the service providers named above (Google AdMob for ads; our file and video hosting providers to deliver lessons), which are needed for the app to work.

7. Children

Darija Tutor is intended for teenagers and adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Security

Downloads use encrypted connections (HTTPS). Lesson files are stored in the app's private storage on your device.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or privacy laws in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. Because we do not hold personal data about you, most requests are handled by deleting the app's data on your device or through Google's ad settings. For anything else, contact us at zahradevelopper@gmail.com.

10. AI-generated content

The voices and some pictures in the app are generated with artificial intelligence tools. No information about you is sent to these tools by the app.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add new features. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy: zahradevelopper@gmail.com
